# 023 Workers and companions
priority: 23
roles: coder, artist-2d, reviewer
depends_on: 022

## Goal
Three workers and a job board from `workers.json`.

## Acceptance
- Rescue interaction in ruins spawns a survivor (Farmer, Smith, Guard, Scout) who follows to the Holdfast; trust meter from food and quests; leaves if the base falls twice.
- Robot relics (Mule, Maintenance drone, Sentry) repairable at the forge; need power.
- Job board UI: assign a worker to a station; server runs the job (repair, farm, craft, defend, fetch dropped relics).
- Workers can die in a breach; sprites approved for all seven.
