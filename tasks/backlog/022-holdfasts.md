# 022 Holdfasts
priority: 22
roles: coder, artist-2d, reviewer
depends_on: 012, 021

## Goal
A party claims one ruin in the world and builds it up: the Palworld base.

## Acceptance
- Claim flag piece; one Holdfast per party; footprint from the ruin prefab; persisted like the plot.
- Generator socket + cable power network; lamps and turrets only work when powered; the reactor is a horde target.
- Wave pressure at the Holdfast scales with light radius, noise made that day, relics stored (`data/waves.json` modifiers).
- Turret piece firing at Thralls, needs power and scrap ammo.
