# 025 Ruin prefab assembly
priority: 25
roles: artist-2d, coder, reviewer
depends_on: 013

## Goal
Assemble ruins and later cities from hand-authored room prefabs with door and loot sockets.

## Acceptance
- 20 prefabs (shells, interiors, cellars, rooftops) as scenes with `DoorSocket` and `LootSocket` markers; tiles approved.
- Assembler places prefabs on a street grid with loot tier by distance from the Keep; sealed buildings need a hammer or Tinker's kit.
- Dark interiors spawn Wired by day.
