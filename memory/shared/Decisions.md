- Personal, business and game knowledge remain separate, with explicit shared notes.
- One dedicated GPU exists: RTX 3080 Ti 12 GB in the gaming PC. No new GPU planned.
- User expects OpenRouter's 1,000 daily free requests to suffice, based on about 600 in heavy daily use.
- Preserve Reaper's Relics game/world/style documents already in this repository.
- Learning starts with evidence-backed notes and skills; training is optional, not a setup prerequisite.
